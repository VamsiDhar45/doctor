PILES or HEMORRHOID

What are piles?

A group of veins or bunch of veins situated in and around anus region, appears like Bunch of grapes.

Normally, Anal cushions are present which are blood filled spongy structures. These cushions engorged and dragged down during defecation and protrude out as swelling.

Due to excessive Straining, these swelling causes bleeding.

What are the causes for Piles?

The major cause is CONSTIPATION

Constipation define as

  1. Excessive straining during Bowel movement and
  2. Passing hard Stool and
  3. Not passing stool daily and incomplete emptying of Bowel movement.

What are the common signs and symptoms of Piles?

  1. Splash of blood passed immediately after bowel movement, which is usually painless.
  2. Itching around Perianal region
  3. Piles associated wits Pain in conditions like Thrombosed External Hemorrhoids or associated condition like Anal Fissure.
  4. Prolonged bleeding leads to ANEMIA, which cause Fatigue or Easy tiredness.

How to diagnose Piles?

Clinical history and office Procedure like PROCTOSCOPY ( Visualizing Anus and lower Rectum ) helps in diagnosis and grading of piles.

What are grades of Piles?

There are four grades

  1. Grade -1: bleed but do not prolapse.
  2. Grade -2: prolapse outside the anal canal but swelling reduces spontaneously.
  3. Grade _3: swelling appears during bowel movement and it needs manual reduction.
  4. Grave -4: complete prolapse of piles and not reducible.

What is treatment for piles?

  1. Grade-1 and some Conditions of Grade-2 can be managed with conservatively without doing Surgery.
  2. Grade-3 and 4 require surgery.

What are the Surgical options for Piles?

  1. Open hemorrhoidectomy: Hemorrhoid tissue is excised & wound left open.
  2. Stapler hemorrhoidectomy : No wound outside and day care Procedure.
  3. Hemorrhoid tissue removed using stapler devise

LASER Haemorrhoidectomy:

Laser used to shrink Pile mass

  1. No pain
  2. No wound
  3. No bleeding
  4. Day care procedure.
  5. Early recovery
  6. Can be discharged on same day

What is Post operative care after surgery for Piles?

  1. Avoiding constipation
  2. Modify lifestyle & dietary habits
  3. High fibre intake
  4. Laxatives

ANAL FISSURE

What is Anal fissure?

It is a cut or break in inner lining at outlet of Anal canal

What are the common causes of Anal fissure?

  1. CONSTIPATION
  2. Prolonged diarrhoea
  3. Obesity & Poor diet
  4. Trauma

What are the common signs and symptoms of Anal fissure?

Severe, excruciating pain during bowel movement associated with streaks of blood passed along with stool. Pain Persist for 1-2 hours after bowel movement

How to diagnose Anal fissure?

Perianal examination helps in visualizing and diagnosing fissure.

How to treat Anal fissure?

  1. Avoid constipation
  2. High fiber diet and intake of more water
  3. Exercise
  4. Medication for stool softening like Laxatives
  5. Sitz bath
  6. Ointments or Creams for local application
  7. Pain killers

Majority of patients can be treated without surgery

Surgery indicated for chronic fissure or Fissure not subsided with medications.

What is the SURGERY for Anal fissure

Lateral anal internal Sphincterotomy

LASER treatment for Anal fissure

  1. No Pain
  2. No cut
  3. No bleeding
  4. Early recovery
  5. Same day discharge

FISTULA IN ANO

What is Fistula in Ano?

Boil or Pustule around anal region that discharges pus or fluid.

Normally, Anal glands situated in anal mucosa secrete mucus. Infection of Anal glands leads to formation of Abscess that burrows deep through skin and forms fistulous opening.

Few diseases like Crohn's disease, Tuberculosis, STD, Diverticulitis etc. also forms fistula.

What is the treatment for Fistula in Ano?

Surgery is permanent cure and it has to be addressed from deep.

Fistulectomy with or without Seton placement

LASER Procedure which is minimally invasive without major wound and without discomfort.

LASER Procedure is painless, minimal bleed, sphincter damage is less, minimal cuts, early recovery and fast healing time.

COLORECTAL CANCER

Includes Colon and Rectum depending upon site of origin of cancer.

What are the common causes of Colorectal cancer?

  1. Advanced age
  2. Polyps in colorectum
  3. Family history or Hereditary
  4. Ulcerative colitis
  5. High fat intake

What are the common symptoms of Colo-rectal cancer?

  1. Right side colon includes Ascending Colon and part of Transverse colon.
    1. Symptoms are weakness / fatigue, Brown or black coloured stool, Pain abdomen, loss of, weight and loss of appetite and Bowel obstruction.
  2. Left side colon cancer symptoms are Bleeding per rectum, Change in bowel Pattern that is diarrhoea alternating with constipation, Abdominal Pain and Symptoms of Bowel obstruction, Etc.

How to diagnose Colo-rectal cancer?

  1. Colonoscopy and Biopsy
  2. CT or PET-CT

What is the treatment for Colo-rectal cancer?

  1. Surgery
  2. Chemotherapy
  3. Radiotherapy
