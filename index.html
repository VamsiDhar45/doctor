Faq's

What can happen if a hernia is not treated?

If not treated, a hernia can grow larger and more painful or can develop complications.

What are the symptoms of liver failure?

Fatigue, Nausea, Loss of appetite, Diarrhea, Vomiting blood, Blood in the stool

Is stomach cancer caused by stress?

Although stress doesn't directly cause stomach cancer, chronic stress may increase cancer risk or make cancer spread faster.

How do doctors check for gastric issues?

Upper gastrointestinal series (UGI), ultrasounds, MRIs, CT scans and X-rays
