Liver Transplant Surgery in Hyderabad

  • Home

  • Liver Transplant

A liver transplant is a surgery performed to replace a failing liver with a healthy one that comes from another person's body. You can get an entire liver from a donor who has recently been pronounced as dead, or you can get a part of a liver from a living donor. A divided liver can come back to full size in both your body and the living donor's body.

When Is a Liver Transplant Required?

Liver failure can happen suddenly because of viral hepatitis, drug-induced injury, or infection. This is known as acute or fulminant hepatic failure.

It can likewise be the end result of a long-term issue. Conditions that can lead to chronic liver failure include:

  1. Chronic hepatitis with cirrhosis
  2. Primary biliary cholangitis, an uncommon condition in which your immune system obliterates your bile ducts
  3. Sclerosing cholangitis, scarring and narrowing of the bile ducts inside and outside your liver, making bile to back up
  4. Biliary atresia, a rare liver disease that influences babies
  5. Overuse of Alcohol
  6. Liver cancers, like hepatocellular carcinoma
  7. Wilson's illness, which leads to unusual levels of copper all through your body, including your liver
  8. Hemochromatosis, in which your body has an excess of iron

Who Can Be a Liver Transplant donor?

A living donor might be your spouse, relative, friend or some well-wisher. They will have complete medical and mental tests to keep risks as low as could be expected. Blood type and body size are pivotal to finding a match. A donor younger than 60 years of age is ideal.

Hospitals will check possible donors for liver issues or different things, including:

  1. Liver disease
  2. Overuse of alcohol or medications
  3. Cancer
  4. Infections
  5. Hepatitis
  6. HIV

What happens at the time of liver transplant operation?

Liver transplants usually require 6 -12 hours. Your specialists will take out your liver and replace it with the donor liver. Since a transplant is a significant procedure, the surgeons will place a few tubes in your body. These tubes are important to assist your body do specific things during the operation and for a few days afterward.

How long can you live after a liver transplant?

This relies upon many factors, including your age, overall health status, and the original liver disease that made you really need a liver transplant. In some cases, that disease might return or proceed. The average survival rate after liver transplant is 75% following 5 years and 53% following 20 years.
